Kanade supports niri and Umbriel and is pre-1.0: expect breaking changes, and read [known gaps](/reference/known-gaps/) for compositor-specific limits before relying on a feature. Umbriel starts Kanade through its autostart list; that direct startup currently has no automatic crash restart.

The design records live in the [repository](https://github.com/0x1d1e/kanade), indexed in [Decisions](/decisions/).
